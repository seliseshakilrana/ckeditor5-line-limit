CKEditor 5 – LineLimit demo

This editor allows at most 16 lines of 40 characters. Long lines wrap automatically and re-flow as you edit. Enter and typing are blocked at the limit, and pasted content is trimmed.

Type here. When a line gets longer than forty characters the last word moves to the next line.
